Trap-setting discovery questions

Open-ended questions that expose the gaps in Snowflake dbt projects and surface the operational risks your champion can take upstairs. Don't open with feature comparisons — open with business value.

Framing principle: The goal is to move the conversation from "what does dbt platform have that Snowflake dbt projects doesn't?" to "what does your data platform need to be ready for what's coming?" Feature differences are the second conversation, not the first. Do not share with Snowflake.
🎯 Lead with curiosity

Ask genuinely. Let them describe their current state before positioning anything.

💬 Listen for the ceiling

The pain usually appears when they say "we have to do that manually" or "we built something custom for that."

📈 Quantify the gap

Follow any "yes, that's a problem" with: "What's the business impact of that today?"

Deployment & orchestration

Cost · reliability · ops burden
Opening frame: "Tell me about what happens when a dbt job runs in production. Where does it live, how is it triggered, and who's responsible for it?"
If model 47 out of 60 in your DAG fails, what happens to the other 59 models the next time the job runs?
Surfaces → Without state-aware orchestration, Snowflake dbt projects reruns all 60 models — even the 59 that didn't change. Platform customers see 30–50% compute reduction with SAO. That's a number you can put in front of a CFO.
Cost
Walk me through how you schedule a dbt job today. What does the setup process look like?
Surfaces → Snowflake dbt projects schedules via Snowflake Tasks, requiring manual SQL authoring with no retry logic, no native alerting, and no history UI.
Dev experienceCost
When a production pipeline fails at 3am, how does your team find out — and what's the typical time to recovery?
Surfaces → Snowflake Tasks have no native alerting. Teams need custom webhooks or Snowflake Alerts to get notified. dbt platform sends native email/Slack alerts with model-level context.
Dev experienceScale
If you need to check whether a job ran successfully last Tuesday, what does that look like today?
Surfaces → Snowflake dbt projects requires SQL queries against TASK_HISTORY. dbt platform provides a full job run history UI with model-level pass/fail, duration, and row counts.
Dev experience
How much engineering time has gone into building and maintaining your current orchestration setup around dbt?
Surfaces → Teams that DIY orchestration around Snowflake Tasks end up rebuilding what dbt platform provides — custom alerting, retry logic, history views. This engineering cost often exceeds platform pricing.
CostScale
Do you check whether your source data is fresh before your dbt jobs kick off? How?
Surfaces → Source freshness monitoring isn't managed in Snowflake dbt projects. dbt platform provides built-in freshness checks as part of the job run lifecycle.

CI/CD & version control

Cost · speed · safety

What happens when a developer opens a pull request today — does anything run automatically to validate the change?
Surfaces → Snowflake dbt projects has no managed CI. Every PR triggers a full DAG rebuild rather than running only the modified models (Slim CI).
CostDev experience
How long does it take to run your full test suite on a pull request? Is that slowing down your engineers?
Surfaces → Without Slimmer CI, every PR runs the full test suite even if only one model changed. Opens the Slim/Slimmer CI conversation and the associated compute cost.
CostScale
What happens when two developers have open PRs at the same time — do they ever conflict or block each other?
Surfaces → Snowflake dbt projects has no concurrent PR isolation. Simultaneous PRs can overwrite each other's work in the same schema.
Dev experienceScale
Before merging a change, can you preview how the model output actually changes — not just that tests pass?
Surfaces → Advanced CI (compare changes) is not available in Snowflake dbt projects. dbt platform can show exact row-level output diffs before a PR is merged.
Governance
How do you handle branch management and merge conflicts in your dbt project today?
Surfaces → Snowflake Workspaces only support pull and push — no branch management, no conflict resolution. Full Git integration is a dbt platform feature.

Has a dbt upgrade ever caused something to break in production? How did you handle it?

Governance

Who can currently deploy to production in your dbt environment — and is there an approval process, or can anyone push?
Surfaces → In Snowflake dbt projects, anyone with the right Snowflake role can execute against production. There's no approval gate, no deployment history, and no record of who made the change that broke the dashboard.
GovernanceSecurity

Security & access control

Compliance · identity · network
When someone leaves the company, how quickly is their dbt access revoked — and is that a manual process?
Surfaces → Without SCIM, deprovisioning is manual. SCIM automates user lifecycle management via the identity provider, ensuring access is removed the moment someone is offboarded.
Security
Is SSO required for your enterprise tools? Is dbt on that list — and if not, should it be?
Surfaces → SSO is an Enterprise-tier feature. Snowflake dbt projects relies on Snowflake's own SSO but doesn't provide dbt-native SAML/OIDC integration.
Security
If your security team asked for six months of audit logs for your dbt environment right now, what would you show them?
Surfaces → Snowflake dbt projects provides no dbt-level audit logs. dbt Enterprise includes 3-month retention; Enterprise+ has unlimited. This is often a compliance requirement.
SecurityGovernance
Do you have compliance requirements that mean data and traffic can't traverse the public internet?
Surfaces → PrivateLink routes all traffic between dbt and your warehouse over a private network connection. Enterprise+ only — and incompatible with Snowflake dbt projects (IP restrictions break the Native App).
Security
How do you control which teams or users can see or modify specific models — is that managed at the warehouse level only?
Surfaces → Snowflake RBAC only applies at the schema level. dbt platform RBAC provides model-level and environment-level permissions that Snowflake can't replicate on its own.
