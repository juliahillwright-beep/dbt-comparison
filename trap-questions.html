Snowflake dbt projects → dbt Enterprise

For prospects running dbt natively inside Snowflake Workspaces via the Snowflake Native App. Key themes: lock-in, compute waste, no managed CI/CD, personal workspaces that don't scale, and no dbt Labs support.

📊

Plan overview & strategy

Lock-in · scale · cost
Opening frame: "Before we get into the product, I'd love to understand where you are today and where you're headed. Mind if I ask a few questions?"
Does your organization run data workloads on any platform other than Snowflake today — or is that something on the roadmap?
Surfaces → Snowflake dbt projects is locked to Snowflake only. Any multi-warehouse strategy, M&A activity, or new platform adoption means rebuilding the entire transformation layer.
Lock-in
Have you ever gone through an acquisition — or is that something leadership thinks about? What happened to the acquired team's data stack?
Surfaces → M&A is the most common way teams end up with a second warehouse unexpectedly. Snowflake lock-in becomes a strategic liability overnight.
