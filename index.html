dbt Labs

How dbt platform compares

A comprehensive feature breakdown across Snowflake dbt projects, dbt Core, dbt Teams (legacy), dbt Starter, dbt Enterprise, and dbt Enterprise+.

Notes on naming & Snowflake projects: dbt Teams is the legacy plan renamed to dbt Starter in August 2023 — legacy customers retain original pricing and unlimited models (fair use). Snowflake dbt projects refers to running dbt natively within Snowflake Workspaces (via the Snowflake Native App) — purchased through the Snowflake Marketplace. It is not a standalone dbt Labs plan; it requires an Enterprise-tier dbt account and is limited to Snowflake as the data platform.
