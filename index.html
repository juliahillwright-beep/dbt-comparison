How dbt platform compares

From Snowflake dbt projects to dbt Core to full platform — understand what each tier includes and when to upgrade.

→ Trap-setting discovery questions
Naming note: The legacy dbt Team plan is no longer sold; dbt Starter (5 developer seats, 1 project, 15,000 models, 2 run slots) is its replacement. Legacy Team customers keep their plan terms (8 seats, unlimited run slots; Team plans created before August 2023 keep unlimited models under fair use). Snowflake dbt Projects is Snowflake's own native experience for running dbt inside Snowsight (Workspaces + DBT PROJECT objects). It is a Snowflake product, not a dbt Labs plan, and does not require a dbt platform account — it is a solid starting point for small, experienced dbt teams on Snowflake, and it asks those teams to build and maintain CI/CD, retry, alerting, and governance themselves. Snowflake is a longstanding dbt Labs partner. Lead with business value, not a feature checklist. Internal use only — do not distribute externally.
Why teams outgrow Snowflake dbt projects
15–30%
Average compute savings with dbt State*
dbt State skips unchanged models on every run. It's available to Snowflake dbt Projects too via a paid dbt Labs integration — the differentiator is Cost Insights proving the savings, automatic retry, and no warehouse held through parse and compile.
8+
Data platforms supported
Snowflake dbt projects is Snowflake-only. M&A or multi-cloud strategy means rebuilding your transformation layer.
2–6 mo
Senior engineering time to build your own CI/CD†
Snowflake runs the compute; the CI/CD pipeline, failure recovery, alerting, and dbt upgrades are the customer's to build and keep running. Free to run ≠ free to operate.
No ref()
No cross-project references
Another team's project is copied in as a local package and the copies drift. Governance is all-or-nothing at the project-object level — a developer with prod grants can build to prod from a workspace.
